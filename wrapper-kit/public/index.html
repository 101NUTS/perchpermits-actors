Help

What is my key?

A code you get once after paying. It is the only thing that identifies your credits, so save it somewhere safe. This browser remembers it, but a new browser or a cleared cache will not.

I lost my key

Reply to your Stripe receipt email with the date of your purchase and we will send it again. We never email it unasked.

How are credits spent?

Five credits for every match returned. A search that returns nothing costs nothing. If a search would cost more than you have, it returns fewer matches instead of failing.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy a pack now, use it next season. There is no subscription and nothing renews on its own.

Where does the data come from?

Public tennis schedules, results, and bookmaker prices at the moment you search, read live. Fair probabilities have the bookmakers' margin removed. Nothing here is betting advice; check your local law before acting on odds.

Refunds and questions

Unused packs are refunded in full within 14 days on request. Write to the address on your receipt.
